What's Inside

Every drop, intentional.

01
Centella Asiatica
Centella asiatica leaf extract

A revered healing botanical used for centuries in Asian skincare. Rich in triterpenoids that dramatically accelerate skin repair and visibly calm redness in minutes.

02
Hyaluronic Acid
Sodium hyaluronate

Multi-weight molecules that penetrate at different skin depths — surface plumping and deep hydration delivered simultaneously — holding up to 1000× their weight in water.

03
Green Tea Extract
Camellia sinensis leaf extract

Packed with polyphenols and EGCG, it neutralises free radicals, controls excess sebum, and delivers a gentle anti-inflammatory action your skin will feel immediately.

04
Niacinamide
Nicotinamide (Vitamin B3)

The multi-tasking vitamin that minimises pores, fades discolouration, boosts ceramide production, and regulates oil — all while being exceptionally gentle on sensitive skin.

05
Ceramides
Ceramide NP, AP, EOP

Lipid molecules that mirror the skin's own barrier structure. Replenish depleted ceramides lost from UV exposure, harsh cleansing, or environmental stress.

06
Vitamin C
Ascorbyl glucoside

A stable, slow-release form of vitamin C that brightens skin tone, stimulates collagen synthesis, and protects against oxidative damage — without the sensitivity of pure ascorbic acid.

Application

How to use
your hero.

The SOS Hero Rescue Spray is designed for every moment — morning ritual, mid-day refresh, or evening recovery. No rules, just skin that always wins.

01
Cleanse & Tone

Begin with clean skin. Pat dry gently — never rub. Your skin barrier starts here.

02
Mist from 20–30cm

Hold the bottle at arm's length and spray in a sweeping motion across the entire face. Eyes closed.

03
Press, Don't Wipe

Use clean fingertips or a soft cloth to gently press the mist into your skin. This activates absorption without disrupting barrier function.

04
Layer & Lock

Follow with your serum, moisturiser, or SPF — or use as a final setting spray over makeup. Re-mist throughout the day whenever skin needs it.
